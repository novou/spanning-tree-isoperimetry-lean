# Formalization project

Formalize the current manuscript in ../spanning-tree-isoperimetry-rewrite. Do not edit that manuscript or inspect unrelated historical research projects. The author requests the whole paper, not merely numerical checks. Work toward that target and report completed and outstanding proof dependencies accurately.

Use Lean 4.34.1 and mathlib v4.34.1 initially. The dependency manifest pins the exact revisions. Keep the local toolchain in .tooling/elan; run Lake through scripts/lake.sh.

Only checked, closed theorems count as formalized. Do not use sorry, sorryAx, ad hoc axioms, native_decide, or unchecked external arithmetic. Standard mathlib axioms such as Classical.choice, propext, and Quot.sound are permitted. Do not disguise missing paper lemmas as hypotheses of a claimed final theorem. Unproved target statements may be definitions of propositions, clearly marked as targets and never counted as completed proofs.

The author explicitly requires the final project not to depend on unproved external results. Lean/mathlib remain permitted. Every additional mathematical result needed by the paper must have a complete kernel-checked proof in the project; a citation, binary artifact, previously registered theorem, or extra hypothesis does not discharge it. The author explicitly permits public proof-code reuse provided complete proofs are included and rechecked locally. Preserve licenses and attribution. The local Foundations library is source, not a trusted external theorem dependency, and counts as checked only after its actual build and axiom audit pass.

Keep compilation efficient: use focused imports and at most two compiler processes across the whole team. Probes and axiom audits count as compiler processes. Coordinate slots through the root agent; use sequential checks for large modules if memory pressure returns. Do not launch broad duplicate builds that overload memory. Prefer dependencies on the main theorem's critical path over accumulating unrelated supporting lemmas. For expensive finite decidable checks, `decide +kernel` avoids duplicate elaborator evaluation while keeping verification in the Lean kernel.

Maintain docs/coverage.md linking manuscript labels to declarations and missing dependencies. Compile each changed module and maintain an axiom audit. Keep scratch files under build/. Use ordinary Git commits, not separate hash inventories. Never claim that compilation of supporting lemmas proves the full classification.

Write explanatory documentation from the current paper and current Lean source, with direct mathematical prose and clear transitions. The author prohibits corrective contrast templates such as “不是 X，而是 Y”, “not X but Y”, “X, not Y”, and “X rather than Y”; express necessary comparisons through each object's relevant properties. Preserve substantive hypotheses, logical negations, normalization details, and proof-route differences. Omit editorial history, irrelevant exclusions, and anticipatory disclaimers; the author explicitly rejects overdefensive writing in documentation as well as in the paper.
